### Positive Trials And Durable-Benefit Limits

<!-- source_subsection_id: SUB013 -->

Positive trials establish where PI3K-alpha inhibition has clinical activity, but they also define the limits of durable control. In SOLAR-1, men and postmenopausal women with HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer whose disease had progressed on or after aromatase inhibitor therapy were assigned to alpelisib 300 mg daily plus fulvestrant or placebo plus fulvestrant after tissue-based PIK3CA testing. In the PIK3CA-mutated cohort, median progression-free survival was 11.0 months with alpelisib-fulvestrant versus 5.7 months with placebo-fulvestrant, with a hazard ratio of 0.65, and objective response and clinical benefit rates also favored the alpelisib arm. That result supports clinical activity in a genomically selected endocrine-therapy context, not a claim that resistance is solved: progressive disease remained the major reason for stopping treatment, and on-target or class-consistent toxicities such as hyperglycemia, rash, gastrointestinal toxicity, dose modification, and permanent discontinuation shaped real treatment exposure `pmid-31091374`.

Longer follow-up sharpened the same message. The final SOLAR-1 overall-survival analysis reported median OS of 39.3 months with alpelisib-fulvestrant versus 31.4 months with placebo-fulvestrant, but the prespecified O'Brien-Fleming boundary was not crossed. The trial also enrolled before CDK4/6 inhibitors became routine first-line therapy, leaving only 20 PIK3CA-mutated patients with prior CDK4/6 inhibitor exposure. SOLAR-1 therefore remains a strong positive PFS trial and a weaker source for contemporary sequencing, post-CDK4/6 durability, or mechanism-of-resistance claims `pmid-33246021`.

BYLieve addressed part of that sequencing gap by prospectively testing alpelisib plus fulvestrant after immediate prior CDK4/6 inhibitor plus aromatase inhibitor. Cohort A met its 6-month progression-free endpoint and had median PFS near 7.3 months, but it was phase 2, open-label, and non-comparative. The later report underscores the durability limit: by the July 29, 2022 cutoff, 126 of 127 enrolled patients had discontinued treatment, most often because of progressive disease, with adverse events another major contributor. Its value for a resistance review is therefore concrete but bounded: it shows activity after CDK4/6 inhibitor exposure while leaving unanswered which resistant clones, pathway bypasses, or endocrine-escape states drove later progression `pmid-33794206` `pmid-39637900`. A matched real-world comparison favored BYLieve over standard real-world treatments after CDK4/6 inhibitor plus aromatase inhibitor, but the comparison was not randomized and real-world progression assessment was less standardized than RECIST-based trial assessment `pmid-33909934`.

INAVO120 extends the positive-trial landscape to a first-line triplet in endocrine-resistant, PIK3CA-mutated HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer. Inavolisib plus palbociclib-fulvestrant improved overall survival versus placebo plus palbociclib-fulvestrant, 34.0 versus 27.0 months, and maintained a progression-free survival advantage, while increasing selected adverse events including hyperglycemia, stomatitis or mucosal inflammation, diarrhea, rash, ocular toxicity, and grade 3 or 4 events `pmid-40454641`. Even here, the clinical result should be treated as an efficacy and benefit-risk object rather than as direct proof of a single resistance mechanism. Exploratory SOLAR-1 genomics further argues for caution: PFS benefit was seen broadly in PIK3CA-altered disease, but co-alterations such as MYC or RAD21 were associated with limited benefit in small exploratory subgroups, without multiplicity-adjusted confirmation `pmid-41967638`. Mechanistic papers in the packet provide the needed conceptual boundary: paired biopsies and experimental models suggest heterogeneous resistance, including PIM upregulation or persistent FOXM1-linked biology, but those data belong in mechanism sections unless a trial directly embeds specimen evidence `pmid-27604488` `pmid-32976773`. Thus, positive PI3K-alpha trials should be abstracted with treatment line, prior CDK4/6 exposure, endocrine context, mutation assay, response and survival endpoints, safety management, dose intensity, and subgroup analyses, because durability limits are visible clinically before their biology is fully assigned.
