## Rewritten Text

Metabolic and microenvironmental escape are supported by the packet, but the evidence is uneven and should be written as a set of specific tested dependencies rather than as a broad resistance category. The most direct metabolic example concerns PTEN-loss, anti-HER2-resistant breast cancer cells. In HCC1569 cells, CD36 was higher than in anti-HER2-sensitive SK-BR-3 cells and HER2-negative MDA-MB-231 cells. Blocking exogenous fatty-acid uptake by serum deprivation or by the CD36 inhibitor SSO reduced proliferation, and SSO further enhanced the anti-proliferative activity of alpelisib and inavolisib. The authors connect this to an adipocyte-enriched tumor microenvironment as a plausible source of free fatty acids, but the direct experiments are in cell lines and combination assays rather than patient samples under PI3K-alpha inhibitor selection `pmid-39920872`.

A second metabolism-linked mechanism comes from NF1 loss. In a genome-wide PiggyBac transposon screen and resistance screen in PIK3CA H1047R mammary tumor models, Nf1 loss emerged in tumors resistant to alpelisib. NF1 depletion in PIK3CA H1047R breast cancer cell lines and a patient-derived organoid reduced sensitivity to PI3K-alpha inhibition and correlated with enhanced glycolysis and lower ROS. Unexpectedly, N-acetylcysteine sensitized NF1 knockout cells to alpelisib and reverted the glycolytic phenotype; phosphoproteomics suggested stronger mTOR pathway inhibition with the combination. This supports redox and glycolytic state as a resistance-associated vulnerability, while the exact NAC mechanism remains unresolved `pmid-37044095`. Metabolic imaging work adds a biomarker angle: persistent FOXM1 after PI3K-alpha inhibition was associated with resistance in ER-positive breast cancer models, drove LDHA rather than HK-II, and enabled detection of resistant metabolism by hyperpolarized pyruvate-to-lactate exchange rather than FDG trapping `pmid-32976773`.

Growth-factor and systemic metabolic feedback also matter. BYL719-resistant breast cancer cell lines showed increased IGF1R, IRS1 or IRS2, and p85 phosphorylation, formation of an IGF1R-IRS-p85-p110beta complex, and persistent AKT-mTOR-S6K signaling. Insulin withdrawal was sufficient to abrogate resistance, and IGF1R or p110beta inhibition circumvented resistance, tying extracellular insulin or IGF input to PI3K-alpha escape but also raising tolerability concerns for dual metabolic-pathway inhibition `pmid-27048245`. In patients, the clinical evidence is thinner: a four-patient real-life alpelisib report monitored hyperinsulinemia and used pioglitazone in selected patients, arguing from preclinical evidence that insulin control could preserve efficacy, but without randomized causal testing `pmid-37163178`.

Microenvironmental evidence is similarly specific. CD44-HA interaction in luminal breast cancer models activated Src-ERK and maintained AKT and mTOR activity during BYL719 treatment; hyaluronidase or HAS inhibition partially restored sensitivity, directly linking an extracellular-matrix ligand interaction to reduced PI3K-alpha inhibitor response `pmid-33024087`. Outside breast cancer, PI3K-pathway inhibitor-resistant lymphoma models upregulated IL-6 and STAT signaling `pmid-31601188`, 3D HNSCC models implicated ECM alterations and beta1 integrin in alpelisib unresponsiveness during radiochemosensitization `pmid-38286037`, and multiple myeloma models showed BYL719 could overcome bone marrow stroma-induced resistance to proteasome-inhibitor contexts `pmid-24405121`. These studies broaden the concept but should be labeled context and hypothesis-generating for PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance in solid tumors.

## Evidence Use Notes

The rewrite converts the draft's broad metabolic and microenvironmental framing into named mechanisms: CD36 and fatty-acid uptake, NF1-associated glycolysis and redox state, FOXM1-LDHA metabolic imaging, insulin or IGF1R-p110beta signaling, CD44-HA interaction, cytokine signaling, ECM or beta1 integrin, and bone marrow stroma. It explicitly labels non-breast and non-PI3K-alpha-selective studies as contextual.

## Residual Uncertainty

The packet contains several strong preclinical mechanisms but limited patient-derived proof that metabolic or microenvironmental escape drives progression during PI3K-alpha inhibitor therapy. Clinical support is especially thin for hyperinsulinemia management, adipocyte-derived fatty-acid uptake, cytokine loops, and ECM adhesion as causal acquired-resistance mechanisms in patients.
