### RTK, HER-family, IGF1R, And MAPK/ERK Bypass

<!-- source_subsection_id: SUB008 -->

RTK, HER-family, IGF1R, and MAPK-ERK bypass should be written as a family of context-dependent escape routes rather than as one generic "RTK activation" claim. In breast cancer, the most direct alpha-selective evidence comes from work asking why BYL719 responses vary across PIK3CA-mutant models. BYL719 inhibited AKT and ERK phosphorylation in sensitive T47D and MCF7 cells, but enforced RAS or EGFR activation prevented ERK suppression while AKT remained inhibited. Ligand and inhibitor experiments then showed that EGFR and HER3 could route signals into both PI3K and MAPK pathways, whereas IGF-1R signaling was more PI3K-dependent in that model. Across 22 breast cancer cell lines and three breast cancer PDX models, high EGFR, c-MET, or FGFR1 expression tracked with preserved ERK signaling and relative BYL719 resistance, while the ability of BYL719 to suppress both AKT and ERK tracked with greater sensitivity `pmid-28382169`.

The IGF1R story is different and should not be collapsed into the same MAPK-bypass bucket. In chronic BYL719-resistant T47D and MCF7 models, resistance was linked to insulin or IGF1R-dependent tyrosine phosphorylation of IRS1 or IRS2 and p85, recruitment of p110beta, and AKT-mTOR-S6K activation. Removing insulin, blocking IGF1R with AEW541, or inhibiting p110beta restored BYL719 sensitivity; the p110beta combination was also active in MCF7 xenografts `pmid-27048245`. Thus, IGF1R can behave as a p110beta-mediated PI3K-pathway compensation mechanism, not merely as parallel MAPK signaling.

Other RTKs enter through lineage-specific mechanisms. In HNSCC and esophageal SCC, acquired BYL719 resistance was mediated by AXL up-regulation, AXL-EGFR interaction, and PLCgamma-PKC signaling that sustained mTOR output despite PI3K-AKT inhibition. Combined PI3Kalpha inhibition with EGFR, AXL, or PKC blockade reverted resistance in these models, and AXL overexpression was observed in resistant laboratory and patient tumors `pmid-25873175`. A separate HNSCC resistance study found that alpelisib-resistant cell lines and PDXs used TAM-family receptor changes with MAPK signaling, reinforcing the idea that receptor up-regulation and MAPK activation can converge on mTORC1-S6, but that the exact mediator varies by tumor lineage and model `pmid-33059733`.

FGFR1 provides a luminal breast cancer example that bridges RTK bypass with endocrine crosstalk. FGFR1 overexpression in MCF7 and T47D cells increased viability, colony formation, and S-phase accumulation after alpelisib; knockdown reversed the phenotype. The resistant state retained Akt, Erk1/2, S6K, Rb, and ERalpha phosphorylation, and alpelisib plus the FGFR inhibitor AZD4547, with or without fulvestrant, produced stronger growth inhibition than alpelisib alone `pmid-40510030`. HER-family evidence is more mixed. A phase I trial of alpelisib, trastuzumab, and the HER3 antibody LJM716 in PIK3CA-mutant HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer tested a rational feedback-blockade strategy, but dose-limiting diarrhea and other overlapping toxicities constrained the regimen and the study does not prove reversal of acquired alpelisib resistance `pmid-33947692`. Reverse-direction HER2 studies, in which p110alpha inhibition helps overcome lapatinib or trastuzumab resistance, are useful for crosstalk biology but should be labeled as HER2-therapy resistance rather than PI3Kalpha-inhibitor resistance `pmid-24249715`.

For clinical interpretation, co-altered RTK pathways may modify PI3K dependence without proving a bypass mechanism in an individual progressing tumor. A taselisib plus fulvestrant correlative analysis proposed that subclonal multiple PIK3CA-mutant tumors may require additional RTK or non-PIK3CA pathway inputs, supporting rational combinations but also underscoring that baseline co-alteration is not the same as acquired escape after alpelisib `pmid-37101291`. Later claim verification should therefore demand drug exposure, timing of rebound, pathway readout, and rescue by the matched RTK, HER-family, IGF1R, MEK-ERK, or downstream effector inhibitor.
