### AKT/mTOR Output Persistence

<!-- source_subsection_id: SUB007 -->

Persistent AKT-mTOR output should be treated as a pharmacodynamic phenotype, not as a single resistance mechanism. The strongest packet evidence comes from PIK3CA-mutant breast cancer models exposed chronically to BYL719, where resistant T47D and MCF7 derivatives retained growth despite continued p110alpha inhibition. In those cells, pAKT suppression was similar in parental and resistant lines, yet S6 phosphorylation fell much less in resistant cells, indicating that downstream mTOR output could persist even when the proximal AKT readout looked inhibited. Phosphotyrosine proteomics then connected this phenotype to increased IGF1R, IRS1 or IRS2, and p85 phosphorylation, co-immunoprecipitation of an IGF1R-IRS-p85-p110beta complex, and functional rescue by IGF1R or p110beta inhibitors in vitro and in MCF7 xenografts `pmid-27048245`.

That separation between proximal pathway inhibition and distal output is also supported by earlier breast cancer work using cell lines, xenografts, and patient biopsies. In PIK3CA-mutant models treated with BYL719, sensitivity correlated with full mTORC1 suppression, whereas resistant models showed persistent pS6 despite inhibited Akt phosphorylation. In MCF7 xenografts, pAkt and pS6 were suppressed early, but pS6 returned after prolonged treatment while pAkt remained inhibited; adding RAD001 or genetically perturbing mTOR-complex components restored sensitivity. This paper is especially useful for later verification because it ties timing, pS6 pharmacodynamics, treatment exposure, and rescue experiments together `pmid-23903756`.

Other models show that the maintained output need not pass through canonical AKT reactivation. A gain-of-function resistance screen and acquired T47D resistance model found that PIM kinases maintained downstream PRAS40, RPS6, and BAD phosphorylation while AKT S473 phosphorylation stayed suppressed under PI3K inhibitor treatment; combined PIM and PI3K inhibition overcame this resistance `pmid-27604488`. In squamous carcinomas, BYL719-resistant HNSCC and esophageal SCC models maintained mTOR activity through an AXL-EGFR-PLCgamma-PKC axis, again despite PI3K-AKT inhibition, and combined PI3Kalpha inhibition with EGFR, AXL, or PKC blockade reverted resistance `pmid-25873175`. A separate HNSCC study sharpened the timing requirement: alpelisib rapidly inhibited PRAS40, S6K, and RSK, but pathway phosphorylation partially or completely rebounded by 24 hours; tipifarnib, through HRAS and RHEB defarnesylation, made mTOR and MAPK inhibition more durable in cell lines, xenografts, PDX models, and an early clinical case `pmid-37339176`.

Later-stage claims should therefore separate at least four categories: IGF1R-p110beta-mediated AKT-mTOR compensation, AKT-independent mTORC1 or S6 persistence, RTK or adhesion-state bypass into mTOR, and genomic reactivation of downstream PI3K pathway nodes. The last category is clinically visible in serial liquid-biopsy and rapid-autopsy analyses of breast cancers progressing on PI3Kalpha inhibitors, where acquired resistance often involved PI3K pathway reactivation, including AKT1 alterations, and double-mutant PIK3CA models could remain sensitive to downstream AKT inhibitors `pmid-37916958`. A CD44-high luminal breast cancer state adds another boundary condition: CD44-hyaluronan signaling activated Src-ERK and maintained AKT and mTOR activity during PI3Kalpha inhibition, with sensitivity restored by disrupting the CD44-HA interaction `pmid-33024087`. Finally, persistent mTORC1 may create vulnerabilities beyond simple vertical inhibition, as PI3K inhibitor-resistant HR-positive breast cancer models with constitutively active mTORC1 were sensitive to metabolic perturbations that produced energy stress and aspartate depletion `pmid-40113784`. The review should avoid using any one of these papers to claim that all pS6 rebound is AKT-driven, or that any PI3K plus AKT or mTOR combination has overcome acquired resistance unless prior PI3Kalpha exposure, rebound timing, and rescue context are explicit.
