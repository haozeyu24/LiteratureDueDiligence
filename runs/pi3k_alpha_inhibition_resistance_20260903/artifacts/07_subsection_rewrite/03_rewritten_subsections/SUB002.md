## Rewritten Text

Alpelisib established that selective PI3K-alpha inhibition can improve outcomes in PIK3CA-mutated HR-positive HER2-negative advanced breast cancer, but the clinical record also makes the durability problem visible. The benefit-risk evidence should be read as a distribution of outcomes rather than as proof that every progression event has the same cause. The FDA and practice literature summarize SOLAR-1 as showing a clinically meaningful PFS advantage for alpelisib plus fulvestrant, while also reporting frequent dose modifications and discontinuations. In one clinical summary of SOLAR-1 safety, 21 percent of patients permanently discontinued alpelisib alone because of adverse events, most often hyperglycemia, rash, diarrhea, and fatigue; these events can reduce exposure without constituting tumor resistance `pmid-33575071`.

The strongest packet evidence for the biologic side of durability comes from longitudinal profiling rather than from the pivotal trial alone. In a phase I/II study of alpelisib plus an aromatase inhibitor in HR-positive metastatic breast cancer, 44 patients were evaluable for clinical benefit, and the observed clinical benefit rate was 52 percent. Paired tumor and ctDNA analyses showed that resistance was not a single lesion: baseline or acquired PTEN loss-of-function alterations were detected in resistant cases, activating ESR1 mutations were associated with lack of benefit or expanded during therapy, and other downstream PI3K and MAPK pathway alterations also emerged. The authors interpreted resistance as convergent and heterogeneous, with tumor evolution and failure of the endocrine partner both able to produce progression on the combination `pmid-32864625`. This supports cautious language: progression after alpelisib can reflect acquired tumor evolution, pre-existing resistant subclones, endocrine partner failure, pathway bypass, or exposure limits, and these alternatives require different evidence.

Real-world data reinforce that approved benefit does not equal uniform durability. In the prospective PRAEGNANT registry analysis of alpelisib plus fulvestrant for PIK3CA-mutated HR-positive HER2-negative advanced breast cancer, median PFS was 5.0 months and median OS was 20.1 months, shorter than pivotal-trial benchmarks. Treatment discontinuation was mainly attributed to tumor progression, but toxicity-driven discontinuation remained common, and the most documented adverse events were hyperglycemia, diarrhea, and rash. The same analysis suggested numerically better PFS with earlier-line use, de novo metastatic disease, and fewer comorbidities, while later-line use and preexisting diabetes appeared linked to worse OS, although subgroups were too small for statistical testing `pmid-41925922`. A post-CDK4/6 inhibitor single-center retrospective comparison likewise reported substantial adverse-event burden in the alpelisib arm, including hyperglycemia, rash, anorexia, and a high discontinuation rate due to adverse effects, underscoring that routine-care dose intensity can differ from trial exposure `pmid-41681940`.

Supportive-care studies should therefore be kept adjacent to, but distinct from, mechanistic resistance claims. Hyperglycemia is an expected on-target pharmacologic effect of PI3K-alpha inhibition because PI3K signaling participates in glucose and insulin homeostasis; the ITACA interim analysis highlights early onset and the possibility of dose reductions, treatment delays, or discontinuation `pmid-40152314`. METALLICA tested prophylactic metformin in a single-arm phase II setting and reported no alpelisib discontinuations owing to hyperglycemia, but diarrhea was frequent and benchmarking relied on SOLAR-1 and BYLieve rather than randomization `pmid-38638399`. A separate hyperglycemia-management cohort identified baseline glycemic and metabolic risk as clinically relevant for monitoring and treatment decisions `pmid-37743730`. In a resistance review, these data should be used to explain apparent efficacy loss from interrupted or reduced exposure, not to relabel toxicity as cancer-cell resistance.

## Evidence Use Notes

The draft's durability framing was retained but made more specific. The rewrite separates tumor-intrinsic or partner-pathway resistance from adverse-event-driven exposure loss, using longitudinal ctDNA evidence for PTEN and ESR1, registry evidence for real-world PFS and discontinuation, and hyperglycemia studies for pharmacologic toxicity management.

## Residual Uncertainty

BYLieve is discussed indirectly through packet papers but not cited as a packet paper. Later claim verification should inspect whether specific discontinuation and dose-intensity claims require direct trial tables rather than review summaries.
