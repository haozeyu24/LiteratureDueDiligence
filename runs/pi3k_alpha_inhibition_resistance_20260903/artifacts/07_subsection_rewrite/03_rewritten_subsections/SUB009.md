## Rewritten Text

Endocrine context is part of the evidence, not a footnote, when interpreting PI3Kalpha inhibition in HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer. The regulatory anchor for alpelisib is alpelisib plus fulvestrant after progression on or after endocrine-based therapy, based on SOLAR-1. In the PIK3CA-mutant cohort, the FDA approval summary reports investigator-assessed median PFS of 11.0 months with alpelisib plus fulvestrant versus 5.7 months with placebo plus fulvestrant, but this should be read as an endocrine-resistant, biomarker-selected setting rather than a clean acquired PI3Kalpha-resistance experiment `pmid-33168657`. A clinical review of SOLAR-1 similarly emphasizes that patients had prior aromatase inhibitor exposure and endocrine-resistant disease, with improved response and clinical benefit in the PIK3CA-mutant cohort `pmid-32782489`.

The prior-CDK4/6 question is a major boundary. SOLAR-1 enrolled very few patients previously treated with CDK4/6 inhibitors; one commentary gives the PIK3CA-mutant prior-CDK4/6 figure as 5.9 percent and frames BYLieve as the prospective attempt to address the now-common sequence of progression after CDK4/6 inhibitor plus endocrine therapy `pmid-32817061`. BYLieve then separated patients by immediate prior therapy. In Cohort A, after CDK4/6 inhibitor plus aromatase inhibitor, updated alpelisib plus fulvestrant results showed median PFS of 7.3 months, 22.2 percent progression-free or alive without death at 18 months, and an objective response rate of 19.0 percent. Cohort C, a later-line population in which about two thirds had prior CDK4/6 inhibitor exposure, had median PFS of 5.6 months `pmid-35348782`. These data support activity after CDK4/6 exposure, but their non-comparative and cohort-specific design means they should not be naively compared with SOLAR-1.

Real-world sequencing studies reinforce that treatment line, endocrine partner, and prior exposure can change what "resistance" means. In the German PRAEGNANT registry, 57 patients received alpelisib plus fulvestrant and 55 had prior CDK4/6 inhibitor therapy, directly reflecting post-CDK4/6 use outside the pivotal trial population `pmid-41925922`. In an MSK retrospective cohort of 115 PIK3CA-mutant HR-positive metastatic breast cancer patients who received both everolimus and alpelisib in sequence, 71 percent had prior CDK4/6 exposure; prior CDK4/6 inhibitor use and greater treatment-line burden were associated with worse time to treatment failure, and endocrine partners differed across sequences `pmid-41620612`. A post-CDK4/6 systematic review and network meta-analysis therefore appropriately cautions that cross-trial comparisons are limited by design and eligibility differences, including whether prior CDK4/6 inhibitor or chemotherapy exposure was required or permitted; it also notes that SOLAR-1 has too little prior-CDK4/6 exposure for reliable conclusions in that setting, while BYLieve was excluded from the network meta-analysis because it was non-comparative `pmid-41079023`.

Endocrine-resistance alterations can coexist with, or masquerade as, PI3Kalpha inhibitor escape. A longitudinal phase I/II study of alpelisib plus an aromatase inhibitor found a 52 percent clinical benefit rate among 44 evaluable patients but identified PTEN loss-of-function mutations in 25 percent of resistant cases and expansion of ESR1 activating mutations during treatment, associated with resistance `pmid-32864625`. BYLieve subgroup reporting found no clear ESR1 interaction in Cohorts A or C, although Cohort B showed a numerical trend toward shorter PFS with ESR1 mutation, supporting the need to track endocrine partner and ESR1 status rather than treating all progression on alpelisib-endocrine therapy as PI3K escape `pmid-35348782`. Earlier alpelisib plus letrozole phase Ib data also belong in this lineage: the regimen was tolerable at 300 mg daily and active in endocrine-refractory ER-positive metastatic breast cancer, especially PIK3CA-mutant disease, but it predates routine first-line CDK4/6 sequencing `pmid-27126994`.

INAVO120 adds another layer rather than a simple replacement comparison. The trial tested inavolisib with palbociclib and fulvestrant in PIK3CA-mutated, endocrine-resistant advanced breast cancer after relapse during or within 12 months of adjuvant endocrine therapy; the overall-survival report describes both PFS and OS benefit, but the regimen, line of therapy, and CDK4/6 exposure context differ from alpelisib plus fulvestrant after metastatic progression `pmid-40454641`. Biomarker reviews in this packet support the conservative bottom line: PIK3CA mutation is the established predictive biomarker for alpelisib, whereas ESR1, PTEN, AKT1, FGFR, KRAS, TP53, and longitudinal ctDNA signals remain exploratory or context-dependent for PI3K inhibitor resistance `pmid-31859350`.

## Evidence Use Notes

The packet shifted this subsection from a simple list of trials to a sequencing framework. SOLAR-1, BYLieve, real-world alpelisib series, everolimus-alpelisib sequencing, and INAVO120 differ in endocrine partner, prior CDK4/6 exposure, treatment line, and whether the PI3K-pathway agent is doublet or triplet therapy. The strongest direct resistance evidence for endocrine crosstalk is the longitudinal alpelisib plus aromatase inhibitor study showing ESR1 mutation expansion and PTEN loss in resistant disease.

## Residual Uncertainty

The original SOLAR-1 NEJM paper listed in the draft was not among the selected packet papers, so this rewrite cites the FDA approval summary and review-level SOLAR-1 descriptions available in the packet. Later verification should retrieve and inspect the original SOLAR-1 and primary BYLieve publications for exact eligibility, prior CDK4/6 subgroup denominators, endocrine-resistance definitions, and subgroup statistics.
