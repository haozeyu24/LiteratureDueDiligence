## Rewritten Text

A practical resistance taxonomy should separate what a biomarker is being asked to do. PIK3CA mutation is primarily an eligibility and predictive biomarker for selected PI3Kalpha inhibitor regimens, but it is not a guarantee of durable response. The biomarker review in this packet emphasizes that, among many proposed markers including PTEN loss, AKT signaling, insulin levels, FDG-PET response, FGFR alterations, KRAS, and TP53, only PIK3CA mutation had established predictive value for alpha-selective or beta-sparing PI3K inhibitors in the advanced breast cancer setting at the time of review, while many other signals were retrospective, exploratory, or assay-dependent `pmid-31859350`. Comprehensive genomic profiling refines the eligibility layer: in a large tissue and liquid biopsy analysis, 20 percent of PIK3CA-mutant breast cancer samples carried pathogenic mutations outside the original SOLAR-1 hotspot set, and patients with those other mutations had longer real-world PFS on alpelisib plus fulvestrant than on fulvestrant alone, supporting broader mutation detection while preserving uncertainty about variant-specific benefit `pmid-36321996`.

Resistance markers need a different evidentiary standard. In trial-translational alpelisib plus aromatase inhibitor data, PTEN loss-of-function alterations were present either at baseline in patients with rapid progression or acquired after treatment, and ESR1 activating mutations expanded during therapy. Those observations support PTEN loss as a PI3Kalpha resistance marker and ESR1 as endocrine-partner escape in the same regimen, rather than treating both as equivalent PI3Kalpha biomarkers `pmid-32864625`. In acquired resistance to orthosteric PI3Kalpha inhibitors, serial ctDNA and rapid autopsy analyses found multiple categories: off-target pathway reactivation through PTEN loss or AKT1 activation, and on-target secondary PIK3CA mutations that changed inhibitor binding. Functional modeling further split therapeutic implications, because some PIK3CA resistance mutations were sensitive to allosteric PI3Kalpha inhibition whereas co-emergent AKT1 alterations required AKT-directed logic `pmid-37916958`.

The taxonomy should also label evidence type. Functional screens and perturbation models can nominate actionable mechanisms, such as PIM kinase upregulation maintaining downstream PI3K effector activity after BYL719, but the paired patient-biopsy support was small and preliminary `pmid-27604488`. Similarly, FGFR1 overexpression in luminal MCF-7 and T47D cells conferred alpelisib resistance with sustained Akt, ERK, S6K, Rb, and ER-alpha phosphorylation, and FGFR1 knockdown or AZD4547 combinations restored sensitivity in vitro; this is strong perturbation/rescue evidence, not yet a validated clinical selection rule `pmid-40510030`.

Clinically, the final review should classify biomarkers along three axes: timing, specimen, and action. Timing distinguishes intrinsic resistance, early adaptive escape, and acquired progression after benefit. Specimen distinguishes archival primary tissue, metastatic biopsy, serial ctDNA, and functional pharmacodynamic readouts such as pAKT, pS6, ER phosphorylation, or metabolic imaging. Action distinguishes eligibility for alpelisib or inavolisib, switching endocrine therapy for ESR1-mutant disease, considering AKT-pathway targeting for PTEN or AKT1 alterations, testing PI3Kalpha inhibitor class switching for secondary PIK3CA mutations, and treating FGFR or other RTK markers as investigational combination hypotheses `pmid-39935426` `pmid-42351448` `pmid-42445743` `pmid-32817061`. Later claim verification should force every biomarker statement to name disease context, drug and partner therapy, sample timing, assay method, endpoint, direction of association, and whether the evidence is predictive, prognostic, mechanistic, or exploratory.

## Evidence Use Notes

The draft taxonomy was retained but made more explicit: eligibility biomarkers, resistance biomarkers, pharmacodynamic readouts, and investigational bypass markers are separated. The rewrite also distinguishes clinical predictive evidence from perturbation/rescue evidence and from review-level sequencing guidance.

## Residual Uncertainty

The packet did not include the 2026 SOLAR-1 genomic determinants paper named in the draft citation register, so that specific biomarker subgroup source was not cited. Several markers listed in the prose, including pAKT, pS6, RTK expression, and ER phosphorylation, remain assay- and model-dependent unless later claim verification finds stronger patient-level evidence.
