### Reading Successful, Failed, And Combination Trials

<!-- source_subsection_id: SUB011 -->

Clinical trials should be read as structured evidence about a regimen in a defined population, not as automatic proof of a resistance mechanism. The strongest positive packet evidence is INAVO120: in patients with PIK3CA-mutated, HR-positive, HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer relapsing during or soon after adjuvant endocrine therapy, adding inavolisib to palbociclib and fulvestrant improved median overall survival from 27.0 to 34.0 months and updated median progression-free survival from 7.3 to 17.2 months. The same report also recorded more hyperglycemia, stomatitis or mucosal inflammation, diarrhea, ocular events, and dose interruptions with inavolisib, even though discontinuation of inavolisib for adverse events was 6.8 percent `pmid-40454641`. That trial supports triple blockade of ER, CDK4/6, and PI3Kalpha in a molecularly selected setting, but its success should not be generalized to all PI3Kalpha combinations or all post-progression disease states.

Real-world and early-phase studies show why the trial-reading taxonomy needs both efficacy and feasibility columns. In the prospective PRAEGNANT real-world cohort, 57 patients with HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer received alpelisib plus fulvestrant, 55 after prior CDK4/6 inhibitor therapy; median PFS was 5.0 months and median OS was 20.1 months, with discontinuation mainly due to tumor progression and documented hyperglycemia, rash, and diarrhea `pmid-41925922`. In HER2-positive, PIK3CA-mutated refractory metastatic breast cancer, a phase I study combined alpelisib with trastuzumab and the HER3 antibody LJM716 because preclinical work suggested PI3K blockade could trigger HER3 feedback. The study found maximum tolerated alpelisib doses for continuous and intermittent schedules, but grade 3 or higher gastrointestinal and electrolyte toxicities were frequent, and only 1 of 17 assessed patients had a partial response `pmid-33947692`. A trial can therefore test a rational resistance idea and still yield a constrained clinical path because of tolerability, dose intensity, population, or partner-drug issues.

Translational trial studies are especially useful when they connect outcomes to serial specimens. Alpelisib plus aromatase inhibitor therapy produced clinical benefit in a phase I/II HR-positive metastatic breast cancer cohort, but paired tumor and ctDNA analyses showed PTEN loss-of-function events and ESR1 expansion that could explain resistance to either the PI3Kalpha or endocrine component `pmid-32864625`. By contrast, functional-screen work with BYL719 and PIM kinases gained clinical relevance from paired biopsies, yet the authors explicitly described the biopsy analysis as preliminary because only a small number of paired treatment-naive and post-relapse samples were available `pmid-27604488`.

Combination rationale should be labeled by intended problem: endocrine escape, cell-cycle escape, pathway rebound, vertical PI3K-pathway blockade, parallel RTK blockade, or exposure management. Reviews in the packet help map this landscape but should be treated as interpretive rather than primary proof. They note that post-CDK4/6 evidence is heterogeneous across trial design, biomarker criteria, endpoints, and control arms `pmid-42351448` `pmid-33976551`; that only some PI3K inhibitor biomarker findings are established while many combination biomarkers remain exploratory `pmid-31859350`; that ER-targeted therapy may remain biologically relevant in CDK4/6-resistant preclinical models `pmid-31852484`; and that ctDNA can guide selection but does not, by itself, prove a survival benefit for every genomically directed choice `pmid-39839709`. Later claim verification should extract phase, regimen, line of therapy, biomarker assay, endpoint, adverse-event burden, and whether the trial authors actually framed the regimen as overcoming resistance.

#### Residual Uncertainty

The packet did not include the SOLAR-1 primary report or FDA approval summary named in the draft citation register, so those were not cited. Negative or failed PI3Kalpha-specific trials are represented mainly through review synthesis and the toxicity-limited phase I HER2-positive study rather than a definitive failed phase III primary paper.
