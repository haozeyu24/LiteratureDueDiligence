### PIK3CA Alteration As Entry Point, Not Resistance Explanation

<!-- source_subsection_id: SUB001 -->

PIK3CA alteration is best treated as the clinical gateway into PI3K-alpha-directed therapy, not as an explanation for either sensitivity or resistance by itself. In the regulatory evidence base for alpelisib, the FDA approval summary describes SOLAR-1 as a randomized, double-blind trial of alpelisib plus fulvestrant versus placebo plus fulvestrant in postmenopausal women and men with HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer after endocrine therapy. The primary efficacy population was the PIK3CA-mutated cohort, where investigator-assessed median progression-free survival was 11.0 months with alpelisib plus fulvestrant versus 5.7 months with placebo plus fulvestrant. That result made PIK3CA mutation status, as detected by an approved assay, a treatment-selection biomarker for the approved regimen rather than a resistance mechanism in its own right `pmid-33168657`.

The biomarker boundary is important because the PIK3CA-mutated label compresses several sources of heterogeneity. A real-world comprehensive genomic profiling analysis of 33,977 tissue biopsies and 1,587 liquid biopsies from advanced breast cancer found PIK3CA mutations in 35 percent of tissue samples, including a substantial group with pathogenic mutations outside the 11 SOLAR-1 prespecified substitutions. In the linked clinico-genomic cohort, alpelisib plus fulvestrant was associated with longer real-world PFS than fulvestrant alone in HR-positive HER2-negative disease, including patients with non-SOLAR-1 pathogenic PIK3CA mutations, but the authors also emphasized retrospective design, evolving standards of care, and confounding by line of therapy and prior CDK4/6 inhibitor exposure `pmid-36321996`. Thus, noncanonical activating PIK3CA variants may expand eligibility discussions, but they still do not prove that a tumor is uniformly PI3K-alpha dependent or destined to respond durably.

Testing method and sample context add another layer. A methodologic study comparing targeted NGS and RT-PCR in FFPE primary and metastatic HR-positive HER2-negative breast cancer samples concluded that both approaches can support PIK3CA testing, while performance depends on nucleic-acid quality, available tumor material, and the breadth of variants captured. The practical implication is that a positive result is a route to a drug-regimen decision, whereas a negative or narrow hotspot result may reflect assay design or sample constraints rather than absence of pathway relevance `pmid-36428975`. A clinical review similarly places PIK3CA testing within treatment planning for locally advanced or metastatic HR-positive HER2-negative disease after endocrine progression, reinforcing the idea that the biomarker functions as an access point for alpelisib rather than a mechanistic diagnosis of resistance `pmid-33575071`.

Clinical data outside the pivotal endocrine doublet also argue against over-simple interpretation. In a phase I/II trial of alpelisib plus nab-paclitaxel in HER2-negative metastatic breast cancer, patients with PIK3CA-mutated disease had higher clinical benefit rate and longer PFS than those without detected PIK3CA mutation, but the single-arm design meant the relative contribution of alpelisib, chemotherapy, subtype, and patient selection could not be separated `pmid-33602685`. In the prospective German PRAEGNANT real-world analysis, alpelisib plus fulvestrant after CDK4/6 inhibitor exposure produced outcomes that were somewhat shorter than pivotal-trial benchmarks, and line of therapy, de novo metastatic presentation, concomitant disease, and diabetes appeared to influence outcomes although subgroups were small `pmid-41925922`. SOLAR-1 subgroup analyses suggest that long-term disease control can occur across patient subsets, but that observation is compatible with a distribution of benefit, not a guarantee for each PIK3CA-positive tumor `pmid-34173298`. A resistance review should therefore separate three categories: biomarker eligibility, evidence of clinical enrichment for benefit, and post-treatment biology such as PTEN loss, ESR1 evolution, pathway reactivation, or drug-exposure limitation.
