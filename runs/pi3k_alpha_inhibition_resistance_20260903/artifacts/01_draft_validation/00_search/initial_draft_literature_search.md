# Initial Draft Literature Search

## Search Scope

Lightweight PubMed-centered search for obvious anchor papers before drafting a verification-ready review scaffold on PI3K-alpha inhibitor resistance in cancer. This search was used only to seed citation registers and reduce empty citation clues. It is not the formal subsection-level PubMed retrieval loop.

- Andre et al., SOLAR-1 alpelisib plus fulvestrant in PIK3CA-mutated HR-positive/HER2-negative advanced breast cancer. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31091374/. Candidate role: approved-agent efficacy and safety anchor.
- Andre et al., final overall survival results from SOLAR-1. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33246021/. Candidate role: mature clinical outcome interpretation.
- Juric et al., convergent PTEN loss and clinical resistance to PI(3)K-alpha inhibition. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25409150/. Candidate role: patient-derived acquired resistance anchor.
- Overall survival with inavolisib in PIK3CA-mutated advanced breast cancer. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40454641/. Candidate role: mature INAVO120 outcome anchor.

- Genomic determinants of response to alpelisib plus fulvestrant in SOLAR-1. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41967638/. Candidate role: biomarker and heterogeneity anchor.
- Extended spectrum of PIK3CA mutations detected in breast carcinoma. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36321996/. Candidate role: PIK3CA testing and noncanonical mutation context.
- Inavolisib review in HR-positive/HER2-negative breast cancer. URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42496151/. Candidate role: background review and citation-discovery anchor, not primary resistance evidence.

## Search Limitations

This lightweight search did not exhaustively screen PubMed, optimize subsection-specific queries, collect full PubMed metadata, retrieve full text, or resolve all investigational-agent literature. RLY-2608 and STX-478 may have important recent preclinical, preprint, or meeting-abstract evidence not fully represented in this quick search. Later stages must perform formal PubMed retrieval, semantic abstract review, full-text handling, evidence packet construction, claim verification, and expert inspection.
